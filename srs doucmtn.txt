🛠️ FIXMATE – Complete System Documentation
1️⃣ SYSTEM OVERVIEW

FixMate is a service marketplace platform connecting:

Customers (need services)

Workers (provide services)

Admin (platform owner)

Business Model:

Customer posts job

Worker accepts

Work completed

Customer pays through app

Platform takes 10% commission

Worker receives 90%

8️⃣ BUSINESS LOGIC RULES

Worker cannot accept multiple overlapping jobs.

Customer cannot cancel after job is in_progress.

Payment must go through platform.

Commission fixed at 10%.

Worker rating updates automatically.

Job auto-cancel if not accepted in 24 hours.
